# ADR NNNN: Title

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN — link it when you write that line

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the conflict, or the failure that made the status quo
untenable. Written so someone who was not here understands the pressure without needing the history.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense, as a property of the system. Specific enough to be violated —
a decision nobody could breach is a description, not a decision.

## Consequences

What follows, including the costs. A consequence that is only an advantage is an advertisement.
State what this makes harder, what it forecloses, and what now has to be maintained.

## Alternatives rejected

Each with the reason it lost. This is the section that stops the decision being relitigated from
scratch, and the one a reader reaches for when the constraints change.

## Verification

The command, test or audit that would fail if the decision were violated — or an explicit statement
that there is none, and what that leaves unguarded. A decision with no check is a decision that
drifts.

## Security and privacy

Trust boundaries touched, data at rest or in transit, and what an attacker gains if this is wrong.
"None" is an acceptable answer; an absent section is not.
