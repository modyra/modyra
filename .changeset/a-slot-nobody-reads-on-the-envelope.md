An envelope member no version of the contract has is reported as `MDY_DYNAMIC_UNKNOWN_MEMBER`. A
document reaching for something the contract does not do — a `computations` slot beside the schema,
say — parsed clean, rendered, and quietly did not do it. The check joins the five slots ADR 0097
already covers; a member an *older* version predates keeps its version finding, which is the more
useful sentence.
