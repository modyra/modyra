A key the catalogue declares on a part is not taken from somewhere else.

The pickers' key handler serves both the field and the panel, and it forwarded every press: a key
declared `on: "gridcell"` was answered from the *control* too. `Space` is that key — it commits the
day a calendar is on — so pressing it on the field consumed the press and closed the panel, for a
meaning the contract states nowhere.

It became reachable when the control started opening the panel: before that nobody could stand on the
control with a panel open. A repair that makes a defect reachable is the ordinary shape of a
structural fix, and this is that defect, found by the browser tier in the same run that confirmed the
opening.

The handler asks where the press came from. Inside the panel every declared key is the calendar's;
outside it, only what the widget itself declares is taken. `.mdy-popup` is the primitive class every
popup part carries, so the question is asked of the contract rather than of a kind's own panel by
name.
