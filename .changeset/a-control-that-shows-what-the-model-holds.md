A `@modyra/vue` control shows the value the model holds, however the value got there.

The box showed only what a person had typed into it. A value arriving from anywhere else — a draft
restored, a server correction, a cross-field rule, another control's answer — reached the form and
never the screen. That is the whole of what a field handle is for, and this was the renderer not
delivering it.

It stayed invisible while nothing else displayed the value. The moment the slider's readout began
working, the widget started contradicting itself: the number said 53 while the thumb sat at 50 —
worse than the empty readout it had just replaced, and found by measuring the repair twice instead of
once.

Both directions are held. A control bound to its model can fight the person using it, so the bench
also types into the box and asserts that what was half-written survives: a field made obedient at the
cost of being usable would fail there.
