A multiselect says which value changed

The contract composes these sentences and publishes the words in five languages; three renderers say
them. This one drew the region that carries them and never filled it, so every act on a control whose
whole job is holding several things at once was reported as the empty string — the diagnostics in the
register quote that emptiness directly: *the control said "", which names no value at all*.

`multiselectAnnouncement` is now asked what changed, and it is asked with **both** sides: what was
said last and what is held now. Given only the present it would recite the whole selection every time
anything moved, which is a state and not a change.

A pass that describes no change leaves the region alone. Writing "" over a sentence takes it back
before a reader has reached it, and a second pass over the same state is an ordinary thing for a
renderer to do.

The other two policies — the sentence for a chip that moved, and the one for a quantity that settled
or reached its floor — are the same shape and are not in this change. They are named here so the
next reader knows the region is filled for one act and not yet for the others.
